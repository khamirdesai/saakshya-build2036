Primary walkthrough

Recorded 28 September 2026. Actual application actions and licensed Mumbai footage with prior pipeline analysis.

What it demonstrates

  • Camera onboarding and operator handoff.
  • Detection and plate-read overlays replayed against the footage clock.
  • Search, an explicitly synthetic route example and evidence review.

What to keep in mind

The overlays are analysis replay. The route example uses a synthetic rendered corpus. This film does not establish live inference speed or a government cross-camera route.

Source and scope

These three films retain the original footage, narration and source labels. No new government camera session or Indore deployment is claimed.
