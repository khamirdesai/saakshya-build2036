Sentinel live-stream demonstration

Sentinel network-stream session recorded 28 September 2026, 12:41–12:47 IST. The sandbox streamed prerecorded scenes.

What it demonstrates

  • Live multi-camera wall, focused video and camera intelligence.
  • Government observation search, plate-read gallery and ANPR CSV export.
  • GIS, trace report, evidence review, registry and system status.

Recording context

The session used four selected deep-inference slots and produced 4,465 observations and 200 plate reads. During the filmed take, 6–13 cameras advanced at once. Sentinel delivers recorded camera scenes through its network live-stream sandbox.
